SELECTED WORK

Projects

A selection of research implementations and software projects addressing challenges in privacy, safety, digital trust and application of AI in various domains.

01Digital commerce

TrustLock

Online buyers and sellers need a safer way to complete transactions. TrustLock holds payment until delivery is confirmed and both sides are satisfied.

02Privacy research

Vertical Federated Learning Privacy Defense

Federated models can expose private labels through their outputs. This work combines adversarial training and differential privacy to reduce inference risk while preserving model utility.

03Event operations

YMR Accommodation Booking

A booking and accommodation management system for a large annual retreat, covering lodges, reservations, payments, check-in, and staff operations.

04Health technology

Heart disease classification app

A web-based health information system with an XGBoost model for heart disease risk prediction, appointments, and clinical operations.

05Community care

Envoys Retention App

A mobile-first follow-up tool that helps community teams coordinate care for visitors, new members, and people who need ongoing support.
