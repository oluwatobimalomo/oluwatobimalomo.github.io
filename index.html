Research

I study how machine-learning systems can remain useful while protecting the people and data they depend on. My work focuses on trustworthy and privacy-preserving AI, especially federated learning, adversarial machine learning, and cybersecurity.

My graduate research at the University of Lagos examined defenses for vertical federated learning, combining adversarial training and differential privacy to reduce label-inference risks while preserving model utility. I am interested in how these methods can support responsible AI in healthcare, finance, and other data-sensitive settings.
